1. **Technical Recruiters & Talent Partners**:
   - *Needs*: Immediate identification of core stack within 3–5 seconds (Next.js, Flutter, React, Node.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, MongoDB), current availability, location, and clear contact links.
   - *Friction to Remove*: Vague tech lists, buried contact info, unclear seniority/focus.
2. **Engineering Leads & CTOs**:
   - *Needs*: Evidence of architectural thinking, clean separation of concerns, reliable API contracts, mobile state management discipline, and database modeling.
   - *Friction to Remove*: "Jack of all trades" without depth; replaced with clear architectural tiers.
3. **Startup Founders & Clients**:
   - *Needs*: High velocity, complete end-to-end product ownership (from responsive web dashboards to cross-platform mobile apps), and low-friction communication.
